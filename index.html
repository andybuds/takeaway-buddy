🛒 Asia Market Ballymount

Asia Market Ballymount Pantry & Best Brands

Specific brand recommendations to hit exact takeaway flavors at the lowest cost per gram:

  • MSG (Monosodium Glutamate) Ajinomoto
    Buy 1kg bag at Asia Market (<€5). Standard supermarkets rarely sell pure MSG.
  • Takeaway Curry Paste Goldfish Brand
    Essential 405g tub for authentic 3-in-1 curry gravy.
  • Indian Ground Spices & Kasuri Methi TRS / MDH
    400g bags of Garam Masala & Kashmiri Chilli give deep color without excess burn.
  • Chilli Crisp & Szechuan Pastes Lao Gan Ma / Lee Kum Kee
    Crispy Chili in Oil (Lao Gan Ma) and Toban Djan (Lee Kum Kee) for Firecracker & Dan Dan.
  • Shaoxing Rice Wine Pagoda Brand
    Red bottle. Essential for meat velveting and wok aroma splashes.
  • Soy Sauces & Black Rice Vinegar Pearl River Bridge / LKK
    Light Soy (PRB), Dark Soy (LKK), and Chinkiang Black Rice Vinegar.

⚡ Electric Hob Masterclass

1. Flat-Bottomed Pan: Use flat carbon steel woks to retain 70% more heat contact.

2. 5-Minute Preheat: Crank the electric ring to max 5 minutes before cooking.

3. Single Batch Rule: Cook 150g–200g portions max to avoid stewing.

4. Two-Ring Setup: One ring on MAX for searing, second ring on LOW for curries/gravy.
